Info & Policies
Company guidelines, operational documentation & HR policies
📋 Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves, 12 sick leaves, and 20 earned leaves per year. Leave must be applied at least 2 days in advance for planned absences. Emergency leaves must be informed within 2 hours of absence.
🏠 Work From Home Policy
Effective September 1, 2026: Employees may WFH on Wednesdays and Fridays. Attendance must be logged digitally. All-hands meetings and performance reviews require in-office presence.
💸 Expense Reimbursement Policy
Travel and business expenses up to ₹5,000 can be claimed within 7 days with receipts. Above ₹5,000 requires department head approval. Claims are processed in the next payroll cycle.
🔐 Data Privacy & Security Policy
All employee data is confidential and governed by IT security guidelines. Access to HR systems requires 2FA. Sharing employee records externally is strictly prohibited without written HR authorisation.
📈 Performance Appraisal Cycle
Appraisals are conducted quarterly (March, June, September, December). KPIs are set at the start of each quarter. Self-assessment forms must be submitted 5 days before the review date.
👔 Code of Conduct
All employees must maintain professional conduct at all times. Harassment, discrimination, and workplace misconduct are strictly prohibited. Violations must be reported to HR within 24 hours.
